1. Report inventory

Catalog every report: regulatory (Call Report, CRA, BSA/SAR, HMDA), risk (credit, liquidity, capital/ALCO), board/management, operational/ad hoc
Tag each by criticality tier — regulatory > board > management > operational — since controls scale with tier

2. Ownership

Per report: a business owner, a data owner (source system), a control owner (validates the pipeline)
One reporting committee for sign-off on new/changed reports and error escalation

3. Lineage & definitions

Source-to-report lineage for every regulatory/risk report — examiners increasingly expect this even at sub-$50B banks (BCBS 239 principles get pushed down informally)
One business glossary — single definition per metric, no report-specific math hiding in a spreadsheet

4. Production monitoring (the "what monitoring" part)

Timeliness — SLA/deadline per report, alert on missed cutoff
Completeness — record counts, expected vs. actual volume
Accuracy — reconciliation to general ledger / core banking system of record
Data quality gate before publish — nulls, ranges, referential integrity
Automated alerting on threshold breach or pipeline failure
Exception log with remediation tracking, not just a Slack message that gets lost

5. Change management

Version control on report logic/calculations
UAT and sign-off before any logic change hits production
Change log tied back to the lineage doc

6. Attestation & audit trail

Preparer → reviewer → approver sign-off per submission, segregation of duties enforced
Retained evidence per report: source snapshot, calc, final output, approvals — retention matched to your exam cycle
Independent (second-line or internal audit) validation on a rotating basis for high-tier reports
